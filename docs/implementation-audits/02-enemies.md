# Hybrid Enemy Integration Audit

**Audit scope:** Reclaimed Breacher, Graft Runner, CHOIR Siren, Ossuary Crawler, Seraph Carrier, and Pale Engine.
**Repository state:** synchronized `main` at `9f6e127`.
**Approved contract:** `docs/PROJECT_CHOIR_STORY_PROPOSAL.md`, especially lines 86–100 and 193–197.

## Executive finding

The existing enemy stack is a strong fit for these six hybrids **only if they remain catalog-driven shells in the five existing procedural family pools**. `EnemyArchetypeCatalog.PROFILES` configures `ProceduralEnemy`; `EncounterRuntime` prewarms family shells and reconfigures them on acquisition; `DistrictResponseDirector` reserves family capacity before scheduling authored `EnemySpawnEntry` records. This already supplies bounded nodes, deterministic targeting, projectile/telegraph reservations, role/trait compatibility, pooled remains, and seeded encounter variation.

The safest shape is therefore to add six IDs and six transparent sprites, extend `ProceduralEnemy` with a small set of explicit states/actions, and add one bounded coordinator (suggested `HybridEnemyRuntime`) for cross-system effects: Graft marks/panic, Siren weapon disruption/decoys, Ossuary containment release, Seraph bay/payload state, and Pale Engine wreck claims. Do **not** create six bespoke enemy scenes, new global enemy pools, unreserved carrier/event spawns, or mutate upgrade ranks to simulate disruption.

A critical distinction in the current architecture is that there is **one six-act `DistrictDefinition`** (`game/resources/siege/district_contact.tres`) used throughout a spatial five-district city. Spatial escalation is supplied by `DistrictPressureCatalog.effective_profile(current_district_id, player_level)`, while `DistrictRecipe` currently only rotates/reverses beats. Hybrid district eligibility therefore needs a new deterministic encounter-pool layer; appending all six directly to the base acts would make Residential/Entertainment/Military promises accidental rather than guaranteed.

## Encounter pools and district recipe shape

`DistrictRecipe` is not presently a district recipe in the spatial-content sense; its fields are `recipe_id`, beat order controls, and catalyst positions. A minimal compatible extension is:

1. Add `HybridEncounterPoolCatalog`, returning read-only weighted substitutions by `district_id`, act ID/threshold, and role/family constraint.
2. Extend `DistrictRecipe` with `hybrid_pool_key` or a small set of per-family weight rotations. The three existing recipes then vary composition while retaining the same content eligibility.
3. Add `HybridEncounterResolver.resolve_beat(base_beat, district_id, recipe_id, run_seed, cycle, act_index, beat_index) -> DistrictBeat`. It duplicates the beat and selected entries, never mutating `district_contact.tres`.
4. Invoke resolution in `DistrictResponseDirector._try_start_next_beat()` **before** `_planned_threat()`, `counts_for_beat()`, and reservation. Store the resolved beat for `_beat_pending`; tests can then assert the exact trace.
5. Guarantee narrative first appearances with fixed insertion rules rather than random weights: first Residential eligible beat contains Breacher; first subsequent Residential pack beat contains Runner; first Entertainment eligible beat contains Siren and one marked containment cell permits Crawler; first Military eligible beats stage Seraph then Pale. Weighted substitution applies only after those introductions.

Recommended pool eligibility:

| Spatial district | Required introduction and pool | Compatibility constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Business | No hybrids | Preserve conventional teaching and current pressure ceiling 8. |
| Residential | Breacher, Graft Runner | Ceiling 11; do not combine doubled Breacher plus a full Runner pack unless authored maximum threat and infantry/light reservations pass. |
| Entertainment | Siren, Crawler; Runner support allowed | Ceiling 14; Crawler event capacity must be included in live-threat planning even though it originates from destruction. |
| Military | Seraph, Pale Engine, Runner | Ceiling 17; Seraph payload reservations and Pale’s threat must leave room for the readable controller relationship. |
| Royal | All mature hybrids, Pale capped at one | Ceiling 20; avoid Siren + Seraph overlapping marks/disruptions unless mark refresh is non-stacking. |

## Art and presentation contract

Current procedural sprites are one standalone **sRGBA PNG with transparency** each, loaded from `res://art/city/enemies/archetypes/`, then uniformly fit to catalog `display`; runtime creates one `Sprite2D` per family shell and swaps only `texture`, scale, collision rectangle, and metadata. Existing sources span 264×192 to 584×516 and 7,093–55,149 bytes; all 20 total about 529 KiB, while the entire enemy art directory is about 797 KiB.

The six new deliverables should therefore be transparent orthographic cutouts with one readable facing, no baked background/shadow/text, no animation atlas, and silhouette-safe padding. Catalog must supply `faces_right`, `display`, `collision`, and `spawn_y`; ground art must sit on the current `LAND_BASELINE_Y`/`LAND_VEHICLE_BASELINE_Y` fitting rules. Recommended rendered envelopes are Breacher 108 px human height; Runner/Crawler 90–125 px high and 150–210 px wide; Siren 140–180 px; Seraph 280–330 px wide and 170–210 px high; Pale 260–330 px wide and 220–260 px high. Collision should cover the solid chassis, not halos, wings, tendrils, or telegraph effects.

Cyan veins, Siren ring, Carrier bay glow, weak-point exposure, Graft mark, and Pale spine charge should be procedural modulation or fixed-cap lightweight presenters, not extra full-size source textures. Add an enemy-art validation test that loads each path, requires alpha, positive/non-oversized dimensions, unique paths, and catalog display/collision bounds. Extend `selftest/enemy_variety_scenario.gd` to exercise and capture all 26; rename count-specific checks. The latest certified Web PCK recorded in repository docs is 14,109,168 bytes against 16,777,216, leaving 2,668,048 bytes. Treat ≤350 KiB total imported delta for six sprites/presenters as a planning target and run the authoritative `game/verify.sh full`; do not ship the high-resolution proposal concept JPGs.

## Runtime budget requirements

The production goal is **zero additional enemy actor shells**. Existing exact capacities are infantry 12, light 3, heavy 4, air 4, siege 2; hostile projectile partitions are bullets 16, shells 4, rockets 4; telegraphs 12; pending beat records 24; actor reservations 9; wrecks 4. Six new catalog IDs do not change `RuntimeBudget.PROCEDURAL_ENEMIES` because shells are family-shared.

Add only bounded ancillary capacities if the effects cannot be drawn by existing shell visuals: `HYBRID_EVENT_RECORDS` (suggest 2), `CHOIR_RING_SLOTS` (1), `MEMORY_DECOY_SLOTS` (3), and `WRECK_CLAIMS` (at most `WRECKS`). Include totals, active/peak counts, denial counts, and post-warm creation counts in `RuntimeBudget.snapshot()`/`validation_errors()`. Ensure these slots are prewarmed before gameplay, rebased on floating-origin shifts, paused with `RunPauseCoordinator`, and reset on retry/continue.

Carrier children and containment releases are the largest compatibility risk because the current `CapacityReservationLedger` accounts only authored beat copies. Extend reservation ownership rather than raising pools. Event threat must also participate in `DistrictResponseDirector._planned_threat()` or an equivalent side-budget, otherwise the 8/11/14/17/20 live-threat ceilings become inaccurate.

## Deterministic test plan

1. **Catalog exhaustiveness:** assert exactly 26 unique IDs/profiles, valid unique texture paths, valid existing family, positive stats, supported behavior/movement/attack style, alpha-clean texture, bounded dimensions, and a unique or explicitly approved behavior signature. Exhaustive facing, expected first district, remains family, baseline TTK, and threat tables must include all six.
2. **Family-shell reuse:** acquire/release an old and new archetype in every affected family and assert identical shell instance ID, changed texture/profile, `post_warm_creation_count == 0`, clean metadata, and zero retained special state.
3. **Breacher state trace:** fixed positions and manual `advance` calls produce sprint → brace telegraph → planted strike → recovery; dash-through changes rear exposure; frontal non-smash reduction and planted `ground_smash` break are exact; cancellation/release produces zero telegraphs/reservations.
4. **Runner controller contract:** unmarked Runner cannot leap; fixed Siren/Carrier mark enables one leap; controller death immediately enters deterministic panic; mark refresh does not stack duration; three Runners saturate exactly the light family and release cleanly.
5. **Siren disruption:** with known ranked weapons, fixed attack sequence picks the expected weapon; only that runtime stops firing while player movement/smash and other weapons continue; menu pause can overlap and release without clearing disruption; expiry restores fire; retry resets disruption but not via rank mutation. Ring and decoys remain within exact slot caps.
6. **Crawler release idempotence:** destroy a marked `(stream_object_id,column,row)` under a fixed seed and assert one reserved Crawler spawn at the exact point; replay callback, stream slot reassignment, and chain callbacks cannot duplicate it; unmarked/business cells produce none; saturated light/event capacity yields one deterministic denial and no pending leak.
7. **Seraph batch reservation:** open bay state is telegraphed; sufficient capacity reserves/deploys exactly three behind-player Runners with fixed offsets; insufficient capacity follows the documented cancellation/partial rule and cannot spin pending forever; killing/releasing Carrier cancels all unused reservations. Test payload destruction with the explicit jab/charge contract.
8. **Pale wreck arbitration:** spawn known wreck slots, two Engines request simultaneously, and deterministic distance/stable-slot order assigns each wreck once; consumption removes/recycles through `EnemyRemainsFactory`, increases capped armor exactly, armor absorbs damage before health, firing lane is snapshotted, and owner death/reuse clears claims. Force a fifth wreck recycle while claimed and assert safe invalidation.
9. **District pool trace:** for fixed seeds/recipes/cycles, traverse chunks 0, 8, 16, 24, 32 and assert Business has none; Residential guarantees Breacher/Runner; Entertainment guarantees Siren/Crawler eligibility; Military guarantees Seraph/Pale; Royal permits mature mix. Same seed trace is byte-for-byte equal, alternate seed varies only weighted choices, and base `district_contact.tres` remains unchanged.
10. **Capacity/threat validation:** every resolved beat passes `DistrictRecipeValidator`; pending actor records ≤24, reservation peak ≤9 (or an explicitly revised cap), family caps hold, event threats remain below the 8/11/14/17/20 district ceilings, and unique-controller caps hold under progression copies/elite assignment.
11. **Projectile saturation:** fill shell/rocket/telegraph partitions and assert Pale/Seraph attacks are denied atomically with no leaked reservation; support telegraphs still obey the 12-record cap and minimum 0.32-second warning.
12. **Remains and targeting:** Siren/Seraph join the exhaustive airborne wreck test and are selectable by `PlayerLaserWeapon`; Breacher uses `SoldierDefeatPool`; Runner/Crawler/Pale use bounded wreck flow; no remains nodes grow.
13. **Lifecycle/floating origin:** after active mark, disruption, containment pending record, Carrier payload reservation, Pale claim, and telegraphs, execute three retries and a cycle continuation.
