# Weapon Shop Concept QA

All five 2560×1440 concepts successfully establish a shared full-screen interstitial grammar: title and district subtitle at upper left, current Rampage Credit at upper right, exactly three centered product cards, an explicit warning that purchases reduce final score, and a prominent Continue action. Product names, prices, and effects are legible and match the planned catalog.

The district differentiation is strong without losing family resemblance. Business uses cyan ledger framing and vault machinery; Residential uses patched teal hardware, chains, water stains, and warm clinic light; Entertainment uses magenta/cyan marquee energy and a dangerous prize-counter composition; Military uses yellow hazard framing, concrete hangar mass, crane rails, and red requisition marks; Royal uses tarnished-gold reliquary frames, crimson velvet, sunburst seals, and cathedral-scale symmetry.

The concepts are design references rather than runtime textures. The shipped Godot overlay will reproduce their hierarchy, palette, card geometry, score warning, and input affordances using compact UI primitives so the Web export remains within its strict PCK budget.
