# Proto Scroller

A Godot 4.7.2 city-destruction slice with a giant robot, five forward-progressing spatial districts, score-funded district weapon shops, 25 six-cell mixed-material destructible buildings, a four-band unobstructed parallax city, destructible props, combined-arms enemies, and a WebAssembly host.

## Engine requirements

Use **Godot 4.7.2-stable** with the matching **4.7.2 non-threaded Web export templates**. The verification harness rejects other engine patch versions, and the Web preset requires both thread support and extension support to remain disabled.

## Project layout

- `game/` — Godot project, launch scene, GUT tests, injected-input scenario, verification entrypoint, and Web export preset.
- `client/` — minimal static edge-to-edge loader that resizes the exported Godot canvas to the complete live browser viewport.
- `client/public/game/` — generated Godot WebAssembly bundle.
- `client/public/title-video/` — silent orientation-specific title loops, static fallbacks, and generation provenance.

## Spatial districts and destructible buildings

Logical-chunk position selects geography independently of campaign access. Business occupies chunks 0–11, Residential 12–23, Entertainment 24–35, Military 36–47, and Royal begins at chunk 48 and continues indefinitely. Each district presents ten destructible-building encounters: two deterministic, independently shuffled passes through its five unique facade types, followed by two road-only transition chunks. Every ordinary street and district remains physically traversable in either direction. Command-boss attempts suspend the rear wall and facade obstruction but temporarily add one player-only boundary 1,000 pixels to the boss's right; the route marker and entire left side remain open, and the boundary drops at body defeat. Clearing a district's tenth facade arms its command boss; defeating and finishing that boss exposes a walk-over salvage marker that opens the matching shop. Closing the shop completes the campaign handoff immediately without requiring eastward travel, rear-frontier culling, or permanent physical containment.

While district progression is held for boss contact, the authored pressure deck recycles instead of exhausting, and reaching each new facade chunk queues one bounded reinforcement beat around the robot, interrupts an empty stale pressure timer, and deduplicates repeat visits; boss interlock acquisition clears the queue and remains the sole stop condition. Bounded act overrun never deletes surviving enemies. If the held battlefield becomes genuinely empty, the director immediately recycles the authored deck and falls back to four doubled-density foot soldiers only when no authored beat can be reserved, so a failed mission cannot blank the screen or strand spawning. The HUD label **SAFE** still refers only to an empty volatile score bank; it does not suppress encounters.

The structural grid uses concrete, glass, and steel profiles. Every failed section now triggers a prewarmed, material-aware impact burst: glass throws fast cyan splinters, concrete blooms into dusty masonry, and steel sheds slower heavy fragments with hot fracture highlights. A short cyan-white flash marks the exact breach, while GPT Image 2 macro-debris continues on the existing physical trajectory and damage path. The **DESTRUCTION** run upgrade now adds two cosmetic debris particles for every baseline debris unit instead of one, while retaining the same fixed 64-instance field and oldest-slot recycling under saturation. Twelve reusable burst slots absorb simultaneous floor and steel-support cascades by recycling the oldest visual instead of allocating during combat. Destroying a full floor starts a staggered upward collapse; destroying every steel cell triggers a faster building-wide support cascade. The player robot rejects self-sourced and player-team damage but remains vulnerable to enemy fire. The giant robot owns world Z 100 and therefore renders above every world-space unit, projectile, particle, prop, debris body, and facade; the HUD remains a separate CanvasLayer overlay.

Defeated soldiers transfer fatal impacts into one intact soldier sprite on a pooled RigidBody2D. The body is thrown and spun by the hit, fades quickly, and is culled back into an eight-object pool; no separated body parts remain. Building rubble is capped at 24 textured bodies, destruction bursts at 12 composite slots, and machinery scrap at 32 bodies. Saturated pools recycle their oldest active effect instead of allocating during combat. The street keeps its physics plane and textured foreground, but no longer draws an opaque rectangle over the lower view.

Destroyed cars, wrecks, defeated soldiers, rubble, and scrap are on nonblocking remains layers, so robot locomotion passes through without shoving them. Surviving light ground vehicles retain 45 percent of normal player knockback while heavy and siege vehicles retain 12 percent, making lighter targets repositionable without turning armored platforms into hockey pucks. Once destroyed, vehicle wrecks switch to a dedicated 110-percent player-attack impulse response with matching angular force; robot attacks can therefore launch wrecks decisively while ordinary hostile or environmental impacts retain the conservative legacy response. Robot attacks still query all remains layers, preserving explosive scattering without turning ordinary walking into an accidental leaf blower.

## Localization

The game ships with English (`en`) and Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`) JSON catalogs in `game/localization/`. The landing-page selector below **Begin Expedition** switches all live title copy and briefing art immediately, then stores a manual EN or ZH-CN choice in `user://localization.cfg` for future launches. Selecting **Auto** clears that manual preference and immediately returns control to Simplified Chinese OS/browser detection, with English as the fallback for every other locale. The Auto control displays its live resolution as **AUTO · EN**, **AUTO · ZH-CN**, or the localized equivalent. Every district shop title, tagline, operator exchange, product name, product description, price/status label, projected stat, confirmation prompt, rejection message, and action is translated into Simplified Chinese and receives the CJK font even though the overlay is created after the gameplay HUD. A deterministic test override is available through `PROTO_SCROLLER_LOCALE=en` or `PROTO_SCROLLER_LOCALE=zh-CN` before launch.

All player-facing copy must be stored as a catalog key and rendered through named placeholders: `L10n.t("hud.health", {"current": "080", "maximum": "100"})`. Resource-authored names, descriptions, and instructions store localization keys rather than English values. Both catalogs must retain identical key sets.
